About

01 — Who I am

QA didn't just teach me to find bugs. It taught me to see the world a little more deeply.

I'm a Software Engineer working in QA at Therap (BD) Ltd. Since January 2024, I've tested complex web and mobile workflows, investigated defects, shared ownership of new features, and collaborated with developers and teammates to improve software quality.
